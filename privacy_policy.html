Privacy Policy for Business Buddy

Application: Business Buddy Package: com.businessbuddy.app Version: 1.0.0 Updated: November 9, 2026

Developer Contact: avishar7051@gmail.com (Project Owner: Avinash Pandit)

1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

Business Buddy is a digital ledger (Bahi Khata), inventory management, and business accounting tool designed for small-to-medium enterprises, shopkeepers, and merchants in India.

We believe that your business transactions, customer debt records, and inventory data belong strictly to you. Business Buddy is built with an Offline-First, Privacy-by-Design architecture under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

2. Information Processed by the Application

A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All business records entered by the merchant remain stored locally on your device within the secure Android application sandbox:

  • Merchant Profile: Business name, contact person, business phone number (optional).
  • Customer Records: Customer name, phone number, and ledger balances.
  • Financial Transactions: Udhar (Gave) and Jama (Got) monetary amounts in Indian Paise, transaction dates, and notes.
  • Inventory & Stock: Product names, SKU/barcode, unit type, selling/cost prices, and stock movements.

3. How Data is Handled & Transmitted

A. Offline-First Local Storage

The App functions completely offline without an active internet connection. All customer accounts and transactions reside in a local SQLite database within the Android OS sandboxed file system.

B. Security & Biometrics

If you enable the Biometric App Lock, authentication is performed directly by Android's hardware-backed Keystore and BiometricPrompt. The App never accesses or stores your fingerprint or facial scan data.

C. Third-Party Integrations

  • Phone Dialer: Opens your device's native dialer with the customer's phone number pre-filled. The App does not place calls automatically.
  • WhatsApp: Formats payment reminders for WhatsApp. The App never sends messages automatically; you must review and confirm before sending.

4. Zero Third-Party Advertising & Trackers

  • Zero Advertising SDKs: No third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob or Unity).
  • Zero Third-Party Trackers: No behavioral trackers or invasive third-party telemetry.
  • No Data Selling: We never sell, rent, or trade your financial records or customer lists.

5. Device Permissions Requested

Permission Purpose Why It Is Needed
INTERNET Network Access Used only if the merchant registers for optional cloud synchronization.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Network Monitor Checks network availability to prevent unnecessary sync retries.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Notifications Allows low-stock alerts and payment reminder notifications on Android 13+.
USE_BIOMETRIC App Lock Protects sensitive business ledger accounts with fingerprint or screen lock.

Note: The App does NOT request runtime permissions for Contacts (READ_CONTACTS), SMS (READ_SMS / SEND_SMS), Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION), or Storage (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE).

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local Data: Stored on your device for as long as the App is installed. You can delete individual records inside the App, clear all data via Android Settings → Apps → Business Buddy → Clear Storage, or uninstall the App.
  • Backend Account Deletion: If you registered a cloud sync account, you can request full data deletion by emailing avishar7051@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Business Buddy is a business management and accounting utility intended exclusively for commercial merchants and shop owners (aged 18 and older). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
