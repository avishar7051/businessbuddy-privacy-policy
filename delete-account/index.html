You can permanently delete your Business Buddy account either directly inside the Android app or by submitting an account deletion request from this public web page without accessing the app.

1. Option A — Delete Immediately Inside the Android App

  1. Open Business Buddy and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Profile → Delete Account (or Settings → Delete Account Permanently).
  3. Review the data deletion and statutory retention summary, check the confirmation box, and type DELETE.
  4. If prompted, reauthenticate with your Google account or account password.
  5. Tap Delete Permanently. Your Firebase authentication identity, sessions, and local encrypted database are cleared immediately.

2. Option B — Request Account Deletion Online (No App Access Required)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, use the request form below or email avishar7051@gmail.com.

3. Identity Verification Process

To protect merchants against unauthorized or fraudulent deletion requests submitted without app sign-in:

  • When submitted from this page, your request receives a unique tracking reference ID (delreq_...).
  • We verify ownership by replying to your registered email address (or verifying via support contact from your registered email/phone) before executing irreversible cloud identity erasure.
  • Verified web deletion requests are completed within 7 days of ownership confirmation.
